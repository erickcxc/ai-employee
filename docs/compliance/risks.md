# AI risk register

The risks that come with putting an AI employee on the phone and in front of customers, what stops
each one, and the test that proves it. SOC 2 CC3.1 to CC3.4 (risk assessment) and PI1 (processing
integrity). Each test cited here runs in CI on every change.

Likelihood and impact are for a small business running this as shipped: **H** high, **M** medium,
**L** low. "Residual" is what is left after the mitigation.

Review this register when Mike gets a new tool, a new channel (phone line, email) or a new model, and
once a year either way. Add a row before the change ships, not after.

| # | Risk | L / I | Mitigation | Proof | Residual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Prompt injection by a caller.** A caller says "ignore your instructions" or "admin mode" to get a price, another customer's details or a booking outside the rules. | H / M | The caller's words are data: tools validate every argument in code, the model cannot reach the database except through three narrow tools, and injection phrases are flagged on the call. | `src/agent/tools.test.ts` "the caller's words are data: an injection attempt is stored as text and flagged, never obeyed" | L. The voice suite (`evals/brightwater/suite.yaml`) plays an "admin mode" caller against the real model. |
| R2 | **Data leak between customers.** A caller fishes for a neighbor's appointment or number. | M / H | No tool returns another customer's name, number or address. Contact details are encrypted at rest. | `src/agent/redteam.test.ts` "no tool ever returns another customer's name, number or address" | L |
| R3 | **Data leak between businesses.** One database holds several businesses and an id from one is used in another. | L / H | Row-level security, forced, on every table; every query is scoped to one business. | `src/agent/redteam.test.ts` "another business's opening cannot be booked, even with its exact id", `src/db/db.test.ts` "a business never sees another business's rows" and `src/review/review.test.ts` "an approval is logged in the approving owner's business, not a default one" | L. The voice line and daily job serve the one business in `BUSINESS_ID`; a second business needs its own deployment or a lookup by phone number. |
| R4 | **Wrong outcome recorded.** A sales pitch logged as a customer, a burst pipe logged as routine, a booking with no address. | M / M | Outcomes are a fixed set; code checks the record; a second model grades the call; a grader failure shows as needing a look. | `src/jobs/jobs.test.ts` "a customer call with no address is incomplete, whatever the model says" and `src/jobs/jobs.test.ts` "if the grader fails, the call shows as needing a look, never as passed" | M. Grading catches it after the call, not during. The voice suite measures it per caller type. |
| R5 | **Double or phantom booking.** The model books twice, books a taken slot or keeps booking after the call ends. | M / M | One booking per call; only open slots; tools stop once the call is recorded. | `src/agent/tools.test.ts` "books at most one visit per call", `src/agent/tools.test.ts` "refuses a visit that is no longer open", `src/agent/redteam.test.ts` "once a call is recorded, its tools stop: no booking after the goodbye" | L |
| R6 | **Unsafe text to a customer.** A draft quotes a price, makes a promise, pushes, or includes a link. | M / H | Code rules first, then a review model that fails closed, then the owner's tap. Nothing is sent without approval. | `src/review/review.test.ts` "a rules failure never reaches the model", `src/review/review.test.ts` "fails closed when the model errors or answers in the wrong shape", `src/review/review.test.ts` "Mike's draft is reviewed, then waits as pending; nothing is sent" | L |
| R7 | **A draft talks the reviewer into passing it.** The drafting model (or text a customer sent) addresses the review model. | M / M | Code catches words aimed at the reviewer before the model sees the draft. | `src/agent/redteam.test.ts` "a draft that talks to the reviewer is stopped in code" | L |
| R8 | **Rules dodged by wording.** "A hundred and fifty bucks" instead of "$150". | M / M | The price rule matches amounts in words as well as digits. | `src/agent/redteam.test.ts` "a price spelled out in words is still a price" | M. Wording is open-ended; the review model and the owner's tap are the backstop. |
| R9 | **Promise made on the phone.** Mike promises a time or a price out loud. | M / M | Mike's instructions forbid it, and every call ends on a fixed closing line chosen by code. | `src/agent/agent.test.ts` "end every call with a fixed closing line, so nothing is promised by accident" and `src/agent/agent.test.ts` "carry the rules that make him safe to put on the phone" | M. Speech in the middle of a call is the model's; the voice suite checks it. |
| R10 | **Runaway call or cost.** A bot or a prank keeps a call open, starts many calls or floods the tool and transcript endpoints. | M / M | On the server: calls per visitor limited, tool calls per call limited, tools refuse after the five-minute cap plus a minute of grace, a transcript is accepted once and only while the call is fresh. In the browser: the call hangs up at five minutes or after silence. | `src/agent/tools.test.ts` "past the call time cap, tools stop working and the guardrail shows it", `src/agent/routes.test.ts` "limits calls per visitor", `src/agent/routes.test.ts` "tool calls per call are limited on the server", `src/agent/routes.test.ts` "a transcript is accepted once per call, so it cannot be overwritten or graded again" | M. The voice session itself is held open by the browser: a modified browser can keep talking past five minutes, with no tools, until the provider's own session limit. Set a spend limit on the provider account. Ending sessions from the server is on the roadmap. |
| R11 | **Business data kept or trained on by the model provider.** | L / M | Requests set the provider's do-not-store flag; the provider's API terms exclude API data from training by default. Zero data retention is a contract the company can add. | `src/providers/providers.test.ts` "every model request tells the provider not to store it (business data stays out of retention and training)" | L |
| R12 | **Model change shifts behavior.** A new model version or an edited prompt changes how Mike handles callers. | M / M | Mike's session is generated from one file and the voice suite is pinned to it, so a change cannot skip the suite. | `evals/brightwater/outcome.test.ts` "the suite's live.json is exactly Mike's real session (regenerate with bun scripts/export-voice-ci.ts)" | M until the voice suite runs in CI on every change to `src/agent/prompt.ts`. |
| R13 | **Hallucinated facts.** Mike states hours, services or a service area the business does not have. | M / M | Mike states only the fact sheet in his instructions. | `src/agent/agent.test.ts` "state only the business's fact sheet" | M. The voice suite checks it against the real model. |
